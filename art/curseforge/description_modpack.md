# Sins of the Fallen Empires (modpack)

**Five empires fell. The last city still stands.**

Everything you need to play *Sins of the Fallen Empires*, the dark fantasy RPG campaign, in one click: the mod, the furniture and decoration mods its cities are built with, JEI and a minimap, and a set of performance mods so it runs on a modest PC.

## How to play

1. Install the pack and start it.
2. On the title screen, press **Begin the Journey**: it makes the world of Aetheris.
3. The Night of the Eclipse begins in Sulthari. Choose your Bearer.
4. Open the **quest book** (the book button in your inventory): *First steps* tells you the keys and how to find your way, and every step of the story is there in order, ticking itself as you play.

## What is in the pack

- **Sins of the Fallen Empires**, with GeckoLib and Curios API
- MrCrayfish's Furniture Mod: Refurbished and Framework, Supplementaries and Moonlight Lib, Small Ships: the cities' furniture, flags and ships
- FTB Quests (with FTB Library, FTB Teams and Architectury API): the quest book
- Just Enough Items, Xaero's Minimap
- Performance: Embeddium, Entity Culling, ImmediatelyFast, ModernFix, FerriteCore, Canary, Saturn, Dynamic FPS

## For a modest PC

Tested for a Ryzen 3 3200G, a GTX 1650 and 8 GB of RAM:

- Give the game **4 GB** of memory (not more with 8 GB in the PC).
- Plug the monitor into the graphics card, and set `javaw.exe` to *High performance* in Windows' graphics settings.
- Render distance 10 (8 if needed), simulation distance 6.

## Servers

Install the same mods on the server except the client-only ones (Embeddium, Entity Culling, ImmediatelyFast, Dynamic FPS, Xaero's Minimap), and set `level-type=sofe\:aetheris` in `server.properties` before the first start.

# Sins of the Fallen Empires (modpack, español)

Todo lo necesario para jugar *Sins of the Fallen Empires* en un clic: el mod, los mods de muebles y decoración de sus ciudades, JEI, un minimapa y mods de rendimiento para PCs modestos.

1. Instala el pack y ábrelo.
2. En el menú, pulsa **Comenzar la Aventura**.
3. Empieza la Noche del Eclipse en Sulthari: elige a tu Portador.
4. Abre el **libro de misiones** (el botón del libro en tu inventario): *Primeros pasos* te explica las teclas y cómo orientarte, y cada paso de la historia está ahí en orden y se marca solo mientras juegas.

**PC modesto** (Ryzen 3 3200G, GTX 1650, 8 GB): dale **4 GB** de memoria al juego, conecta el monitor a la tarjeta gráfica y usa distancia de renderizado 10.

**Apoya el proyecto:** el mod es y será siempre gratis y completo. Si te gusta y quieres ayudar a que crezca (más ilustraciones, música propia), puedes hacer una donación voluntaria: [donar con PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/donate/?hosted_button_id=M298QQM56Q83Y).
